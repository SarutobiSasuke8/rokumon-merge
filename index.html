How to forge

Slide. Match. Temper.

  1. Swipe, or press an arrow key. Every tile slides that way.
  2. Two of the same metal become the next tier and score that tier’s value. A tile merges only once per move — four sparks make two embers, not a coal.
  3. A new piece appears after each move. Next shows whether it will be a spark or, less often, an ember.
  4. The Rokumon crest does not combine. The forge cools only when the board is full and nothing matches.
  5. Daily uses today’s date as a shared seed, so everyone draws the same sparks. You get three undos a run.
